English

Ball Sticker operates an internal shop tool ("Ball Sticker Chat") that lets our staff read and reply to messages customers send to our Facebook Pages and LINE Official Account.

Data we receive

  • Messages, images and files you send to our Page or LINE account.
  • Your public profile name and profile picture, and the page-scoped ID provided by Facebook / LINE.

How we use it

  • Only to reply to you, quote prices, confirm orders and send delivery tracking numbers.
  • Only our own staff can see it. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Storage — Data is stored in our private Google Firebase database and kept as long as needed to serve your orders.

Data deletion — To delete your chat data, send the message "delete my data" to our Page, or email info@ballsticker.co.th / call the numbers below. We will delete your messages and profile data from our system within 7 days and confirm back to you.
