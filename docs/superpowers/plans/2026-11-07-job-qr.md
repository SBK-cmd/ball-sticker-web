# SignTools sheet job QR experiment

Goal: one real app job per print sheet, supporting mixed designs/sizes. Reuse app deep link ?tab=confirm&job=<id>. No mobile cutting command in this experiment.

Approved shape: owner selects a queue job before layout. Print one vector QR, job number and sheet index/total per sheet. Reserve footer in packing and trimming. Keep QR out of cut layers; include QR in CM5 print bounds.

Approaches: a reusable tray QR was discussed; owner chose QR generated into sheet at layout time. Use existing qrcode-generator and job queue rather than another backend. Existing app handles the link after employee login.

Constraints: experimental local ZIP only; do not publish beta/stable without release instruction. Illustrator only; fail closed on Corel. No Firebase mutation from QR creation. No customer address/phone or credential in QR. Do not alter paused web-statistics work.

1. Pure helper + tests: validate real app jobs, URL encoding, matrix, reserved footer, mixed sizes/rotations, clipped sheet and scale-factor cases.
2. Panel/queue integration + host vector drawing: explicit job picker; queue shortcut; default QR off. Reserve footer before packing, preview QR, draw printable Job QR layer and grow CM5 bounds. Retain original behavior when off.
3. Offline panel integration and mocked Illustrator geometry tests; build a locally installable experimental ZIP. Actual Illustrator, RIP/printed scan and Graphtec checks remain manual.

Review focus: stale job selection; cm5 auto crop; narrow sheets; Large Canvas scaling; failure cleanup. Tests should cover all five where practical. The physical cut/mark read cannot be proven without Illustrator/Graphtec.

## Result
- Pure/host checks: 8 tests passing with `node --test --test-isolation=none tests/*.test.cjs`.
- Offline browser: queue -> layout shortcut using fake Firestore, no PATCH writes, mixed sizes/counts, footer on every sheet, stale selection invalidation, Large Canvas, standard/CM5 settings, Corel blocked; no page errors.
- Fresh review found QR failures could leave placed copies behind. Regression test drove actual st_stkPlace through a QR exception (RED); track/remove all new production artwork/cut/note items on error (GREEN).
- `node --check` passed for modified panel JS and host.jsx via stdin. Diff check passed with `core.whitespace=cr-at-eol` to preserve Windows installer CRLF.
- Local experimental ZIP `signtools-panel/SignTools-v1.13.52.zip` built, CRC checked, contained scripts compared byte-for-byte to tested source. No push/deploy/release performed.
- Physical Illustrator execution, print/RIP, phone scan on paper, and Graphtec remain unverified. Mobile cutting is a separate design extension, not present.

Follow-on: user authorized actual mobile cutting beta. See 2026-11-07-mobile-cut.md. Paper QR now opens the SignTools mobile cut page; that page retains the app job link. Version 1.13.53 supersedes the QR-only 1.13.52 experiment.
