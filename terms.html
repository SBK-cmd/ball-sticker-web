"Ball Sticker Chat" is an internal tool used only by Ball Sticker staff to reply to customers who message our Facebook Pages and LINE Official Account. It is not offered to the public.

  • Messages you send us are used only to answer your questions, quote prices, confirm orders and send delivery tracking numbers.
  • Prices and order details sent in chat are confirmed by our staff before production.
  • Your data is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
